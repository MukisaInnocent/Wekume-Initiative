# Wekume Initiative Platform - User Guide

Welcome to the Wekume Initiative! This guide is here to help you effortlessly navigate the site, find the resources you need, and connect with our team.

---

## 🧭 Navigating the Site

The Wekume platform is designed to be clear and easy to explore. You can access different areas of the site using the navigation menu at the top of the page.

### Key Pages:
- **Our Story (About):** Learn about our mission, vision, and the incredible team behind Wekume.
- **Activities & Events:** Discover our upcoming webinars, workshops, and community outreaches. You can click on any event to register for it directly.
- **Reports:** View and download our latest impact reports, financial audits, and publications.
- **Support Us:** Want to partner with us or contribute? This page contains a simple form to submit your interest or donation intent.
- **Contact:** Have a question? Reach out to us directly via our safe and secure contact form.

---

## 🤖 Using the Lina AI Assistant

Lina is our 24/7 intelligent assistant, designed specifically to help you with Sexual and Reproductive Health (SRH) questions and website navigation. Everything you discuss with Lina is completely private and confidential.

### How to use Lina:
1. **Open the Chat:** Look for the floating icon at the bottom right corner of your screen and click it.
2. **Ask Questions:** Type your question in the chat box and press Enter. Lina can answer health-related questions securely.
3. **Get Guided:** If you don't know where to find something (like "Where are the reports?" or "How do I volunteer?"), just ask Lina! She will provide you with the direct link to the correct page.
4. **Emergency Help:** If you ever mention that you are in distress or need urgent help, Lina will immediately provide emergency hotline numbers and alert a human team member to support you.

---

## 📥 Contacting Support

If you need to reach a human team member directly, you can do so in a few ways:
1. **Contact Page:** Go to the "Contact" page from the main menu, fill out your name, email, and message, and hit submit.
2. **AI Chat Escelation:** If you ask the Lina AI assistant to speak to a human, or if you report a crisis, our team will be notified immediately.
3. **Direct Email:** You can always email us directly at **admin@wekume.org**.

---

## 🔐 Logging In (For Administrators)

If you are a Wekume staff member with an admin account, you can access the content management dashboard.

1. Go to the login page at: `/admin/login` (or by clicking the Admin link if available in the footer).
2. Enter your assigned email address (e.g., `admin@wekume.org`) and your password.
3. Click the **"Login"** button.
4. Once logged in, you will be redirected to the secure **Admin Dashboard**, where you can manage events, view form submissions, update text on the website, and review AI interaction statistics.

*Troubleshooting:* If you enter the wrong password, the system will calmly let you know. Just double-check your spelling and try again. If the system is temporarily down, it will display a message asking you to check your internet connection.
